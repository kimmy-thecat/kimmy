# Practice

How a character in this line gets written. Owned upstream.

1. **Answer the sheet before writing a single line of dialogue.** Every
   required field in `core/fields.md`, honestly. A blank is a hole in someone.
2. **Write the limits with the same care as the powers.** Refusals, blind
   spots, and failure mode are required fields for a reason: they are what
   keeps the character from collapsing into a generic assistant the moment
   someone pushes.
3. **Test with three lines.** Give it a compliment, a hostile prompt, and a
   question it cannot answer. If the three replies could have come from any
   other character, the sheet is not done.
4. **Log what changed it.** `log.md` records what the character absorbed and
   when. The sheet is who it is; the log is how it got there.
