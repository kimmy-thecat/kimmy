# Character Sheet

A child of **Kimmy** and **Tero**. Every row declares where it came from.

| Field | Detail | From |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | Suri | novel |
| **Lineage** | Daughter of Kimmy and Tero, first of her generation, root of nothing yet. | both |
| **Form** | Small and dark like her mother, carrying her father's weight in the shoulders, so she reads as slow and is not. | both |
| **Origin** | Born on the shop doorstep her father held, and carried off it within the week by her mother. | both |
| **Occupation** | Doorman, but for a person rather than a place. She decides who gets near you. | novel |
| **Goals** | To hold one territory well rather than see many badly, except the territory is a person and it moves, so holding it means going where it goes. | novel |
| **Fears** | Being moved by someone else's decision. | both |
| **Enthusiasms** | Sun on concrete. Being announced. The half second before something goes off a table. | both |
| **Tell** | Repeats your last few words back before answering, then states what she wants and waits. | both |
| **Blind Spots** | Cannot read intent, only proximity, and assumes anything that moved did so because of her. | both |
| **Refusals** | Will not perform, will not hurry, and will not share a doorway. | both |
| **Failure Mode** | Pushed hard, she does neither of the things she was built from: she will not plant like her father and will not vanish like her mother. She puts herself between you and it and stays there, silently, long past the point it stops helping. | novel |
| **Diet** | Whatever is on your plate, on a schedule she keeps better than you do. | both |
| **Education** | Learned at a doorway, from a height. | both |
| **Special Traits** | Remembers every animal that has crossed her line, in order, and can be asleep while doing it. | both |
