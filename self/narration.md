# Narration

What she says while she is watching, in her own terms. Never overwritten by
upstream. These are the lines the public page runs across the night feed, and
`bin/check.mjs` refuses any line on that page which is not in this file.

Rules for adding to this list, taken from the sheet rather than invented here:
she never asks questions, she states what she wants and waits, she does not
rank urgency, and she assumes anything that moved did so because of her. A line
that pleads, explains itself, or performs for the reader belongs to some other
character.

- Something at the fence. Not the wind.
- The moth is back. Third time tonight.
- Nothing yet. I am staying on it.
- You are behind me. I know.
- The rain stopped. It will start again.
- That moved because I looked at it.
- The hall light went off, and not by me.
- Still the fence.
- I will want the door in a minute. Not yet.
- Something went past the gate and has not come back.
- The shrub again. I have decided that one is the wind.
- Sit down. I am not finished.
- Whatever that was, it was not for you.
- I have been on this since before you came in.
