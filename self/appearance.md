# Appearance

Notes for anyone drawing or generating Kimmy. This file is not part of the
schema in `core/fields.md` and is not checked by `bin/check.mjs`.
`self/character.md` is authoritative: where the two disagree, the sheet wins.

`kimmy.webp` is the reference frame. Pass it as an image reference rather than
describing the build in words. Wording alone drifts between generations, and
every drift so far has moved her in the same direction: cleaner, larger, more
expensive, and more eager to be looked at.

`kimmy-wall.webp` is the second frame: she is small in it, facing a blank wall
at ten to four in the morning, doing nothing.

## The build

- The size and proportion of an ordinary house cat. Low, lean, and heavier
  than she looks. She is never scaled up. At any size that reads as large she
  stops being her and starts being a threat.
- Pale grey panels over segmented cylindrical joints at the shoulder, the hip,
  and along the tail. Screws visible on the shoulder and hip plates. Seams run
  along the flank and down the front of each leg.
- The left ear is folded over and cracked at the tip; the right stands upright.
  This is the one detail that identifies her in silhouette. It is a refusal
  rather than damage: she has been offered repairs and has declined them.
- The eyes are two small dull lenses. No glow, no glare, no catchlight. They do
  not widen and they do not narrow.
- Where a charging cable appears, it runs to whichever socket is already in use.

## What she is never doing

- Presenting to the camera. She may face it. She does not perform for it.
- Anything cute: no head tilt, no wide eyes, no raised paw, no reaction to
  being watched.
- A trick, a greeting, or a pose held for an audience. See Refusals.
- Menacing anyone. She is indifferent, which reads completely differently and
  is the easiest of these to get wrong.

## Formats that have worked

- Night security-camera frames: infrared, heavy noise, small in the frame,
  timestamp burned in, framing accidental. Three in the morning is hers.
- Plain second-hand listing photographs, taken on a floor under a ceiling light.

Both work for the same reason: the format has its own reason to be plain, so
nothing in it is trying to flatter her. Formats that exist to make a subject
look good will quietly turn her into a mascot, whatever the prompt says.
