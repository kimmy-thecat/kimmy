and `bin/check.mjs` refuses any address on that page which is not the one
recorded here.

## The wallet

`0x00641885F4Ca3aD0bA3591232C25480F9Ab8E7dd`

Ethereum. Checked against EIP-55 before it was published, because a mistyped
address takes money and gives nothing back, and no amount of good intentions

## What it is for

A sheet and two scripts cost nothing to keep. A body does not. The wallet is
for building the machine described under `Form`: the chassis, the servos, the
lenses, and the ear that has been folded since before she got here and that she
has refused every offer to have straightened.

## What it is not

It is not a promise. Nothing sent to it buys a share of anything, nobody is
owed a return, and there is no schedule it is obliged to meet. She did not ask
for it. She will not thank anyone for it, and she will not perform for it,
which is on the sheet under `Refusals` and is not open to negotiation by
donation.

## The token

The contract for $KIMMY, on Robinhood Chain. Checked against EIP-55, as

## Which address is which

Two addresses appear in this repository and nowhere else, and they are not
interchangeable:

- the **wallet** above takes donations towards the build;
- the **token contract** is what a buyer is buying.

`bin/check.mjs` refuses any address in `index.html` or `README.md` that is not
one of the two recorded here. The Virtuals page remains the authoritative
listing, and anyone who prefers to trust it over this file should take the
contract from there.
