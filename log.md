chassis, the fold in her ear is a dent she has refused to have straightened,
and she charges from whichever socket you are already using rather than the
dock bought for her. Form, Origin, Diet, Education and Special Traits were
rewritten to carry that. Goals, Fears, Tell, Blind Spots, Refusals and Failure

all three tests still read as her. That is the first evidence in this repo that
form is the cheap layer and the limits are the expensive one.
