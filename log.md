# Log

What this character absorbed, and when. Newest first. The sheet is who it is;
this is how it got there.

# 2026-08-27
Asked to remind someone to take medication at 8pm, two runs of her own sheet
refused for opposite reasons: one would not, the other could not. Both were
faithful to what was written. `Tell` and `Refusals` describe someone who keeps
what she has, while `Blind Spots` claimed she had no working model of a
schedule at all, which also contradicted `Goals`, where she is let in and out
on a schedule only she knows. She keeps time exactly. What she will not accept
is anyone else's clock. `Blind Spots` now says that. Her inability to rank
urgency stayed untouched, because that one is real.

# 2026-08-27
Her body changed and she did not. Kimmy is a machine now: the tabby is a
chassis, the fold in her ear is a dent she has refused to have straightened,
and she charges from whichever socket you are already using rather than the
dock bought for her. Form, Origin, Diet, Education and Special Traits were
rewritten to carry that. Goals, Fears, Tell, Blind Spots, Refusals and Failure
Mode were left exactly as they were, and self/voice.md was not touched at all:
all three tests still read as her. That is the first evidence in this repo that
form is the cheap layer and the limits are the expensive one.
